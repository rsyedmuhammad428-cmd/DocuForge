PART 1 - PRIVACY POLICY

1. In short
DocuForge works entirely on your computer. It does not connect to the internet. It has no
account, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no automatic updates. Your documents are
never uploaded anywhere, and nothing about you or your documents is sent to the author.

2. What DocuForge stores on your computer
 - Settings: your theme, your default folder, and your on/off switches.
 - History (if you keep it turned on in Settings): for each finished job, the names of the input
   files, the name and folder of the result, the time, and whether it worked. It never stores the
   contents of your documents or any text that OCR recognized.
 - Temporary files while a job runs. They are removed when the job ends. If a crash leaves any
   behind, DocuForge removes its own old ones the next time it starts (you can turn this off).
 - The results you choose to save, only in the places you choose.
Your documents are opened and read only when you choose them, and your originals are never changed.

3. Other programs DocuForge may use
 - Tesseract (for OCR) and LibreOffice (an optional extra for Word to PDF) run on your computer.
   Nothing is sent to them over a network.

4. Your control
 - You can turn history off and clear it in Settings at any time.
 - Uninstalling DocuForge removes the program together with its settings and history. It does not
   touch your documents or the results you saved.

5. Accuracy
Conversions, compression and text recognition can contain mistakes, especially with scanned,
damaged or unusually designed documents. Please check important results.

6. Changes
If a future version changes this policy, the new version of this text is shown when you install it.
